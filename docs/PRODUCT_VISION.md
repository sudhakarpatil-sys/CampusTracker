# CampusTracker - Product Vision

## Mission

CampusTracker is an AI-powered academic management platform designed to simplify academic life for students, faculty members, and educational institutions.

Instead of managing attendance, timetables, assignments, notes, and announcements across multiple platforms, CampusTracker provides one unified ecosystem.

---

# Vision

To become the all-in-one academic platform that colleges can deploy with minimal setup.

CampusTracker should reduce administrative work while improving the experience for students and faculty through intelligent automation.
