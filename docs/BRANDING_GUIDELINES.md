## 1. Brand Identity & Logo Mark

CampusTracker's visual identity reflects intelligence, speed, academic excellence, and modern design elegance.

- **Logo Geometry**: A stylized continuous squircle shield embedding an inner lightning pulse emblem (representing speed and real-time attendance tracking).
- **Primary Brand Color**: Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6` / `#7C3AED`).

---

## 2. The 60-30-10 Color Ratio Rule

To maintain visual balance across dark themes:
- **60% Dominant Canvas**: Obsidian Midnight (`#0B0F17`). Provides calm depth and eliminates visual noise.
- **30% Secondary Surfaces**: Elevated Surface Cards (`#141923` / `#181F2E`). Organizes content hierarchy.
- **10% Focal Accent**: Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) & Emerald Green (`#10B981`). Applied exclusively to primary CTAs, active status badges, and progress arcs.

---

## 3. Cross-Platform Consistency Matrix

| Platform Layer | Primary Role | UX Focus | Design Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Web Control Center** (`apps/web`) | Management, Administration, Faculty Uploads, Admin Reports. | Multi-column density, table views, mouse/keyboard shortcuts. | Wide grid layouts, persistent sidebar, detailed data tables. |
| **Mobile App** (`apps/mobile`) | Daily student companion, quick attendance marking, notes reader, assignments. | One-handed thumb ergonomics, fluid touch gestures, instant actions. | Single-column cards, Smart Stack hero, bottom tab bar, bottom sheets. |
| **Native Widgets & Live Activities**| High-glanceability ambient status. | Zero interaction friction, 2-second glance value. | Single contextual card, high contrast typography, pre-computed timelines. |
